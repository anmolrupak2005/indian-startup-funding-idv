# Indian Startup Funding Trends: Analytical Insights & Findings

*Analysis performed on **357 startup funding transactions** across India.*

## Executive Summary of Core Findings

### 1. Geographic Capital Concentration
**Geographic Capital Concentration:** **Bengaluru** leads the ecosystem with **$23,851.2M** (35.3% of all disclosed capital) across 113 recorded transactions.

### 2. Tier-1 vs Tier-2/3 Ecosystem Divide
**Tier-1 vs Tier-2/3 Ecosystem Divide:** Tier-1 cities captured **84.9% of total deal volume** and **90.1% of total venture funding**, showing that early capital remains heavily clustered in major urban hubs.

### 3. Sector Leadership
**Sector Leadership:** **E-Commerce** emerged as the highest funded vertical with **$19,480.3M** raised over 78 deals, driven by rapid consumer smartphone penetration and payments infrastructure.

### 4. Peak Investment Velocity
**Peak Investment Velocity:** The year **2019** witnessed the highest capital deployment (**$16,172.9M**), while deal velocity peaked in **2015** with **69 deals**.

### 5. Funding Skew & Deal Size
**Funding Skew & Deal Size:** Across disclosed rounds (84.3% disclosure rate), the median deal size is **$93.23M**, compared to a mean of **$224.62M**. The largest transaction in the dataset was **Flipkart** raising **$2,385.2M**.

### 6. Prolific Institutional Backers
**Prolific Institutional Backers:** Out of 81 identified investors, **Sequoia Capital India** leads institutional activity with **71 deals**.

### 7. Stage Composition
**Stage Composition:** **Series C+** rounds constitute the largest deal volume slice at **51.8%**, reflecting the volume pyramid of seed and growth investments in India.
